### 1.2 The Five Biological Gates (`Series-System Architecture`)
To function durably in a child with early-onset T1D or an adult with LADA without systemic immunosuppression, an engineered allogeneic 3D `SC-islet` must simultaneously pass **Five Biological Gates**:

1. **Gate 1 — Lineage Purity, Paracrine Cytoarchitecture & Biphasic GSIS Maturity:** Differentiate from pluripotent stem cells (`hPSCs`) into mature `NKX6-1+ / INS+ / MAFA+` $\beta$-cells re-aggregated at physiological tri-hormonal stoichiometry (`60% β : 30% α : 10% δ`, $<1\%$ off-target `SLC18A1+` enterochromaffin cells) capable of dynamic first- and second-phase glucose-stimulated insulin secretion (`GSIS`) and rapid low-glucose shut-off ($<3\text{ min}$ upon $16.7 \to 2.8\text{ mM}$ step-down) without basal insulin leak.
2. **Gate 2 — Adaptive Allo- (`Gate 2A`) & Auto-Immune (`Gate 2B`) Cloaking:** Evade host alloreactive CD8+ and CD4+ T-cells, donor-specific HLA antibodies (`DSA`), **and** pre-existing T1D memory autoreactive T-cells (`PPI`, `GAD65`, `IA-2`, `ZnT8`, `IGRP`) as well as indirect antigen presentation by host `HLA-DQ2/DQ8+` dendritic cells (`APC`s).
3. **Gate 3 — Innate Immune (`NK` / Macrophage) & Thrombo-Inflammatory Shield:** Prevent Natural Killer (`NK`) cell "missing-self" lysis (`CD94/NKG2A`, `KIR2DL1/2/3`, `NKG2C`) and macrophage phagocytosis (`SIRPα` across polymorphic `V1/V2` haplotypes) from **Hour 0** after implantation.
4. **Gate 4 — Intrinsic $\beta$-Cell Cytoprotection (Cytokine, ER-Stress, Granzyme & Amyloid Resilience):** Prevent intrinsic apoptosis, dedifferentiation, unfolded proinsulin ER stress, `Granzyme B` cytotoxicity, and `IAPP` amyloid toxicity under inflammatory (`IFN-γ + IL-1β + TNF-α` / `IFN-α`) and hypoxic stress.
5. **Gate 5 — 72-Hour Vascular/Oxygen Bridge & Quantitative Fail-Safe Erasability:** Prevent central ischemic necrosis during the 72-hour avascular window ($\le 120\,\mu\text{m}$ diameter clusters + isogenic `3:1 iEC:iPericyte` microvessels + 72h `PDMS-CaO2`/Catalase oxygen carrier in a pre-vascularized retrievable site) while maintaining a pan-lineage prodrug suicide switch (`biallelic AAVS1-[HNRNPA2B1-proximal 455-bp CpG-core candidate (GRCh38 chr7:26200580-26201034) (legacy CBX3-A2UCOE alias; anti-silencing function pending)]-EEF1A1-core-iCasp9`) with a quantitatively bounded clonogenic escape probability.

### 2.1 Regulatory Pathway for Personalised & Platform Follow-On Cell Banks (`21 U.S.C. § 356k` & Phase 2b `KEYNOTE-942` / Phase 3 `INTerpath-001` Lessons — `R16`, `AUDIT-REF`, `AUDIT-REF`)
A key regulatory question for immunologically stratified cell therapies (`Stage 5` and `Scenario B`) is how follow-on HLA-C-retained or endotype-tailored cell banks can leverage the manufacturing package of the universal off-the-shelf lead (`Scenario A`). Two statutory and clinical-trial realities govern this pathway (`R16`):
1. **Statutory Scope of FDA Platform Technology Designation (`21 U.S.C. § 356k`, Enacted by `§ 2503` of the PREVENT Pandemics Act, Pub. L. 117-328):** Under `21 U.S.C. § 356k(b)` (eligibility criteria), `§ 356k(c)–(d)` (designation request and determination), `§ 356k(f)` (leveraging data from a designated platform technology across subsequent `21 U.S.C. § 355(b)` NDA or `42 U.S.C. § 262(a)` BLA applications; `§ 356k(e)` concerns actions to expedite development and review of an application using a designated platform; requests and determinations are governed by § 356k(c)-(d)), and the FDA May 2024 Draft Guidance (*Platform Technology Designations for Drug Development*), Platform Technology Designation is available **only after an initial drug or biological product incorporating the platform technology has been approved under an NDA or BLA**, and `§ 356k(f)` enables subsequent applications by the **same sponsor**—or by **another sponsor granted a full contractual right of reference** (alongside `21 CFR § 312.23(b)` right-of-reference cross-referencing at the `IND` stage)—to **leverage shared CMC, nonclinical toxicology, and manufacturing validation data** (such as the standardized `Stage 0->7 SC-islet differentiation + sequential HiFi-SpCas9/AsCas12a HDR + 3-timepoint >=63,000x duplex-seq/CAST-Seq QC` platform) rather than merging genetically distinct allogeneic master cell banks into a single unapproved IND (`AUDIT-REF`).
2. **Distinguishing Patient-Specific Algorithmic Drug Design (`KEYNOTE-942` & `INTerpath-001`) from Modular Allogeneic Cell Banks (`R16`, `AUDIT-REF`):** In patient-specific mRNA neoantigen therapy (`intismeran autogene` / `V940` / `mRNA-4157`), Phase 2b `KEYNOTE-942` (`Weber et al. 2024 PMID:38246194`) demonstrated a primary-analysis recurrence-free survival (`RFS`) hazard ratio of **`HR = 0.561`** (`95% CI 0.309–1.017`, `18-month RFS 79% vs 62%`) and distant metastasis-free survival **`DMFS HR = 0.347`** (`95% CI 0.145–0.828`; with subsequent 3-year ASCO follow-up reporting 3-year RFS `HR = 0.51` and DMFS `HR = 0.384`), and in 2026 the Phase 3 **`INTerpath-001` (`NCT05933577`)** trial in resected Stage IIB–IV melanoma **met its primary RFS and secondary DMFS endpoints at interim analysis**, illustrating clinical precedence for individualized neoantigen selection within a fixed mRNA manufacturing process (while noting that FDA has not yet issued an approved BLA or formal guidance treating algorithmic multi-locus cell-bank selection as a single licensed biologic). Because each allogeneic edited hPSC Master Cell Bank (`Scenario A` vs `Scenario B`) is a distinct biological drug substance, OpenIslet advances **`Scenario A` (`Universal Lean 4-Locus Core`, $K=4, B=0$)** as a single off-the-shelf master cell bank through `Stages 1–4`, and only triggers **`Stage 5` (`Scenario B`)** follow-on `IND`s/`BLA`s cross-referencing the `Scenario A` Master CMC / Platform file under `21 CFR § 312.23(b)` and `21 U.S.C. § 356k(f)` after `Scenario A` approval and only if patient-subgroup data demonstrate residual innate `KIR2DL` mismatch or `HCMV NKG2C+` escape.
