## 0. Epistemic Discipline, Estimand Disclaimer & `v0.7.0` Changelog

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **What the Multi-Gate Score Is — and What It Is Not:**
> 1. **Not a Calibrated Clinical Survival Probability:** All uncalibrated `89–92%` 1-year human insulin-independence probability claims are permanently retired. Because no multi-edit hypoimmune stem-cell-derived islet (`SC-islet`) has completed a multi-patient 1-year human clinical trial without immunosuppression (the furthest clinical data are $n=1$ recipient reports in Carlsson et al. 2025/2026 [`PMID:40757665`, `PMID:42430558`] for `UP421` primary cadaveric islets in an intramuscular forearm sentinel dose and Wang et al. 2024 [`PMID:39326417`] for autologous `CiPSC-islets` under chronic immunosuppression), **any claim of a calibrated human 1-year survival percentage is statistically unsupported.**
> 2. **Relative Multi-Gate Evidence & Feasibility Index (`[0, 1]`):** `scripts/rank_islet1_stacks.py` (`v0.7.0`) computes a bounded, sign-sensitive, endpoint-oriented, non-broadcasting, and locus/base-edit-penalized **Relative Multi-Gate Evidence & Feasibility Index** across 6 gate factors ($G_1, G_{2\text{A}}, G_{2\text{B}}, G_3, G_4, G_5$, where $G_2 = G_{2\text{A}}\cdot G_{2\text{B}}$):
>    $$\text{Index}(S) = \exp\!\big({-\lambda_K \max(0, K - 4)}\big)\cdot \gamma_B^B \times \big(G_1 \cdot G_{2\text{A}} \cdot G_{2\text{B}} \cdot G_3 \cdot G_4 \cdot G_5\big) \in [0, 1]$$
>    with $\lambda_K = 0.15$ (range `[0.08, 0.25]`) and $\gamma_B = 0.965$ (range `[0.920, 0.985]`). All 46 structural gate-logit entries (`48` AST float literals) and all 12 auxiliary evaluator hyperparameters (`gene_signal_scales_by_gate_and_branch`, `signed_gene_signal_slope = 0.42`, `cd47_gsis_penalty_scale = -0.25`, `lrr_shrinkage_formula`, `effect_direction_weights`, `omics_constraint_weights`, `lower_10pct_cred_bound_hyperparameters`) are explicitly inventoried and dynamically wired in `complete_gate_coefficient_registry`. Across the 2,160-stack grid, **structural priors and Graveyard rules govern macro-tier ranking (`9/10` top-10 membership overlap when empirical gene signals are zeroed; `99.85%` of component variance sum), while empirical gene signals act as local within-tier tie-breakers**.
