# Code of conduct

OpenIslet is for anyone who wants to help cure type 1 diabetes, whatever their background. Everything here happens in public, so please:

- **Argue with the idea, not the person.** Disagree as hard as you like about the science, and say why.
- **Keep personal details out.** Don't post medical or personal information about yourself or anyone else.
- **Be honest about what you know.** Say when you are unsure, and show where a claim comes from.
- **Be welcoming** to people who are new to the science, and to people who use AI to take part.

We may remove spam, personal details, and content that attacks people. When we remove something we say so in public, with the reason. To report someone's behaviour, email [hello@openislet.org](mailto:hello@openislet.org).
